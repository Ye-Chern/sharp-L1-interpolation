## Checks and local derivations

1. **Traces and amplitude (paper.tex:190–224).** Put x = 1/n, y = r². Direct path counts give tr M² = n/2+2, tr M⁴ = 3n/8+7/4, tr D² = n+3/2, tr D⁴ = n+9/8. An involution anticommutes with M and commutes with D, so odd-M traces vanish. In the fourth power, the six mixed trace terms are bounded in sum by 6 tr M² using 0≤D≤I and Hilbert–Schmidt Cauchy–Schwarz. This yields the displayed fourth-trace bound and the displayed polynomial difference. The latter is at least 1/80−x/10+109x²/40, positive on 0≤x≤1/20. Consequently s = tr A⁴/v² ≤31/(20n)<1/12. For weights λ²/v, the map a↦log(1+ξ²a)/a is convex (including its limit at 0), so Jensen gives w≤(1+s v ξ²)^(−1/(2s))≤(1+ξ²/24)^−6. Here v≥1/2 and log(1+sz)/s decreases with s. The opposite inequality w≥exp(−vξ²/2) follows from log(1+z)≤z. No reversal is needed.

2. **Phase and sign expectation (paper.tex:225–247).** Determinant differentiation gives ∂r θ = (ξ/n) tr[D(I+ξ²A²)^−1], nonnegative for ξ≥0 even though D and A need not commute. The phase θ at r=0 vanishes by spectral symmetry. Since 0≤D≤I and v(r) increases for r≥0, integration in r gives 0≤μξ−θ≤rvξ³/n. This costs at most rv I₂/n in the half-line sign integral. Compare the remainder with the Gaussian using w≥exp(−vξ²/2): this inequality helps where sin(μξ)≥0, while the negative part costs at most c r[J(L)+J(2L)]/2. Indeed L=2.85<π/1.1 and μ=cr≤1.1r; on [L,2L], where sin(μξ)<0, |sin(μξ)|/ξ≤μ−π/ξ≤μ/2; after 2L use |sin(μξ)|/ξ≤μ. The factor 2/π from sign inversion and the Gaussian erf term are correct. ## Origin regularity, including eigenvalue ties

For n≥3 the core matrix at t=0 has eigenvalues cos(kπ/(n+2)); at least four have absolute value at least cos(2π/(n+2))≥cos(2π/5)=(√5−1)/4. For |t|≤2⁻²⁴, the perturbation norm is at most |t|, so Weyl leaves these four above 1/4 in modulus. This works uniformly over a two-sided neighborhood of zero and all four augmented matrices; hence |Φ_t(ξ)|≤(1+ξ²/16)^−2 uniformly. Eigenvalue crossings, repeated eigenvalues and a coefficient crossing zero do not spoil this bound. In particular the quadratic form has no atom at zero.

Avoid differentiating individual eigenvalues: for affine Hermitian A(t)=A₀+tD with ||D||≤1, the resolvent of I−iξA has norm ≤1. Determinant differentiation bounds |∂t Φ|≤d|ξ||Φ| and |∂t² Φ|≤(d²+d)ξ²|Φ| uniformly. After division by ξ for sign inversion, the first and second parameter derivatives have integrable majorants respectively dW₀ and (d²+d)|ξ|W₀, W₀=(1+ξ²/16)^−2. Dominated convergence thus gives C² sign expectations even at ties, provided the earlier Hcomp identity holds on this neighborhood. Since ∫₀∞ξW₀ dξ=8, |G''|≤16(d²+d)/π<6d(d+1), and the coefficients in Hcomp give |H'''|≤6(n+1)(n+3)(n+4), exactly the stated M. This supplies the local regularity argument; it does not establish Hcomp itself.

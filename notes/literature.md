# Literature map

The exact question for comparison is the additive inequality on a bounded
interval with the three L¹ norms of derivative orders 0, n and n+1.
The coefficient of order 0 is minimized first. The coefficient of order n+1
is then minimized with the first coefficient fixed.

- [Canonical Sets of Best L1-Approximation](https://oa.tib.eu/renate/server/api/core/bitstreams/e1768ddd-d30c-419b-b6eb-90b4c5d66abf/content),
  Theorem 2.4 and Corollary 2.6, state the Bernstein interpolation
  characterization and the unique minimum-L¹ monic polynomial (2^{-n}U_n)
  on ([-1,1]). This directly matches the polynomial extremal in paper.tex,
  §2. Bibliography [2] and [3] there are leads for the original versions.
- [Allan Pinkus, On L1-Approximation](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511526497)
  develops finite-dimensional L¹ approximation and Chebyshev systems. The
  chapter on T- and WT-systems is a reading entry for the alternation and
  duality argument behind the first constant.
- [Curry and Schoenberg, On Pólya frequency functions IV](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02788653)
  is an original source on fundamental splines. The route to verify next is
  the divided-difference normalization in `Hvalue` and `Hderivative`,
  including the factorial in the uniform-simplex expectation.
- [Alexei Shadrin, The Landau–Kolmogorov inequality revisited](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.7708)
  studies the finite-interval problem in the uniform norm with separate norm
  constraints. It is relevant background for the extremal question.
- [S. K. Bagdasarov, Kolmogorov inequalities for functions in classes W^r H^ω](https://doi.org/10.1070/IM2010v074n02ABEH002486)
  is a sharp additive-inequality lead. Its intermediate derivative in the
  statement of interest carries the uniform norm. The hypotheses also involve
  a modulus of continuity of the highest derivative.

The initial search covered the English terms “additive Landau Kolmogorov”,
“finite interval”, “L1 norm”, “sharp derivative inequalities”, and
“Chebyshev polynomial”. The references above establish prior context for
specific ingredients. The exact lexicographic three-L¹-norm statement
remains unresolved in the literature search. In a later pass, search
first-derivative and bounded-variation specializations, trace Finch's primary
references, and check Russian and approximation-theory references on finite
intervals and best L¹ approximation.
